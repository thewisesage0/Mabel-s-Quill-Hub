About the Author

Hi, I'm Mabel — an author, writer, and storyteller, and the person behind Mabel's Quill Hub. I love turning ideas into stories that feel real, emotional, and easy to connect with. I write across genres, focusing on characters and moments that linger long after reading. Some of my work is published under my pen name, Astra Maye, but storytelling is at the heart of everything I do.

Mabel's Quill Hub is my creative space—a place where stories are shaped with care, curiosity, and heart. Whether you're here to read, explore, or collaborate, you're welcome.
